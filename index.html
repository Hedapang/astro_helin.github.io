THE GALACTIC ECOSYSTEM
01 / SCIENCE

Research interests

Understanding how galaxies acquire, circulate, and expel gas—and how their central black holes interact with the larger galactic ecosystem.

01 — THE HALO

Circumgalactic Medium

Tracing faint, extended hot gas in galaxy halos through deep X-ray observations and stacking analyses, from low-mass disks to Milky Way-like galaxies.

02 — THE FEEDBACK

Galactic Winds & Feedback

Investigating large-scale bubbles, galactic outflows, and the interaction between star formation, nuclear activity, and the surrounding gas.

03 — THE NUCLEUS

Black Holes & AGN

Exploring the connection between galaxy interactions and black hole accretion, including X-ray constraints on active nuclei in close galaxy pairs.

02 / SELECTED WORK

Research highlights

A few recent projects combining X-ray astronomy with optical and radio observations.

GALACTIC FEEDBACK · 2025

Giant bubbles in NGC 6286

We identified a pair of approximately 5 kpc-scale ionized-gas bubbles, accompanied by extraplanar soft X-rays, in the interacting starburst galaxy NGC 6286. Diffuse hot gas is detected to approximately 90 kpc.

Read in The Astrophysical Journal ↗
HOT HALOS · 2025

Hot gas around a low-mass disk galaxy

Using eROSITA and Chandra, we found evidence for extraplanar hot gas around NGC 7793, extending to about 6 kpc—providing observational constraints on gaseous halos at lower galaxy masses.

Read in The Astrophysical Journal ↗
X-RAY STACKING · 2026 PREPRINT

Hot CGM of nearby L* galaxies

Stacked eROSITA X-ray data for a representative nearby galaxy sample reveal diffuse emission beyond 50 kpc and provide constraints on the hot baryon reservoir and its links to feedback.

04 / BACKGROUND

About me

I conduct observational research on the gas and energetic processes associated with galaxy evolution. My work draws on Chandra, eROSITA, and other X-ray facilities, complemented by optical and radio data.

I studied astronomy at Nanjing University and pursued doctoral research under Prof. Zhiyuan Li. My projects span the faint hot circumgalactic medium, nearby-galaxy winds, and accretion onto supermassive black holes.
