# Purple academic design

Visual reference: https://helinhl.github.io/ . Adapted principles: concise introduction with a right-side profile photo, research before the publication list, restrained navigation, readable academic entries. This is an independent design, without reference-site assets or code.

- White page, compact 1020 px reading width, smaller Georgia name heading.
- Deep purple emphasis: #54256f.
- Link and focus color: #713c96.
- Primary heading ink: #392344.
- Body text: #47424c.
- Muted text: #78707e.
- Soft purple labels: #f7f2fa.
- Research figure colors are preserved as scientific data.
- Portrait remains a non-human typographic placeholder.

Edit the color variables at the start of assets/style.css to customize. Responsive layouts and reduced-motion preferences are supported. The existing browser visual-QA limitation in VALIDATION.md still applies.

Layout refinement: About uses a single reading column at every viewport size. Repeated uppercase labels above main headings were removed.

Typography update: headings use Georgia (Times New Roman/serif fallback), while body text, navigation and publication details use Arial (Helvetica Neue/sans-serif fallback). Local editing controls also use Arial.

Image update: the three research interests form a vertical sequence, each followed by a complete image. Four-panel CGM profiles and the AGN chart have larger reading widths; the NGC 6286 panel is capped at 500 px. Figures retain natural aspect ratios and link to full-size originals or PDFs. The real portrait uses a display-only crop and links to the unchanged source photo. Empty elements from the author-edited HTML were cleaned up.

Compact refinement: desktop research figures use a 300 px column beside blank note space, 260 px at intermediate widths and up to 285 px on mobile. No caption/credit sits below an image. About is a single sequence of four full-width bands, with a 210 px topic label and dense content area, separated by fine rules. Publication rows omit abstracts for faster scanning. Georgia headings and Arial body text remain.

Refinement: body uses locally hosted Inter (modern sans-serif approximation of the user’s screenshot), with Georgia headings retained. Body ink is #18121d, muted supporting text is dark purple #35203f, and heading ink is #25132f. Contact email lines share a 14 px / weight 500 style (13 px on mobile). Research note areas now contain grounded, editable English summaries of the CGM, NGC 6286 and galaxy-pair studies.
